Is $16 an Hour a Good Wage?

$16 an hour is below the US median wage but livable in low-cost areas. $16/hr ($33,280/yr) is below the US median hourly wage (~$22/hr). It is livable as a single person in low cost-of-living areas (rural Midwest, parts of the South) but tight in most metro areas.

For context: the US federal minimum wage is $7.25/hr. The US median individual wage is approximately $22/hr (Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2024). $16/hr is 38% below the US median ($22/hr). Social Security considers full retirement credits are earned at any wage above $7,078/quarter.
