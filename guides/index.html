CA

California Payroll Tax Guide 2026

Updated Sep 2026

California has the highest marginal income tax rate in the US at 13.3%, mandatory SDI at 1.1% on all wages, daily overtime rules stricter than federal FLSA, and four separate employer-side payroll taxes. This guide covers every rate you need.

State income tax: 1%–13.3% SDI: 1.1% (no wage cap) Daily overtime: 8 hrs/day ETT: 0.1% + SUTA: 3.4%
TX

Texas Payroll Tax Guide 2026

Updated Sep 2026

Texas has no state income tax, making it one of the most employer-friendly states. But federal FICA taxes, FUTA, and Texas SUTA still apply — and many employers make costly mistakes assuming "no state tax" means no payroll complexity. This guide covers everything.

~1,800 words
8 min read
Source: TWC, IRS
No state income tax FICA still applies SUTA: 2.7% new employer Workers comp: optional
