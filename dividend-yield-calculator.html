Dividend Income vs Growth Investing

Dividend investing works best for: income-focused retirees who need cash flow, investors who want lower volatility, or those building a DRIP portfolio over decades. Growth investing (no dividends) works best for long-term wealth building in tax-advantaged accounts where reinvestment is automatic.

The key advantage of dividend growth stocks: companies that consistently raise their dividend tend to have strong fundamentals and compound wealth through both price appreciation and rising income.
