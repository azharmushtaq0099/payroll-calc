Post-Tax Deductions 2026: Roth 401k, Garnishments, and More

Updated October 2026 · FreePayrollCalc Research

Post-tax deductions are taken from your paycheck after federal, state, and FICA taxes have already been calculated. They reduce your take-home pay but not your taxable income — unlike pre-tax deductions.

Common Post-Tax Deductions

DeductionTax StatusKey Benefit
Roth 401(k) contributionsPost-taxTax-free withdrawals in retirement
Roth IRA contributions (via payroll)Post-taxTax-free growth + withdrawals
Wage garnishments (court-ordered)Post-taxLegally mandated
Union duesPost-taxUnion membership benefits
Life insurance (employee-paid, >$50k coverage)Post-taxCoverage above IRS exclusion limit
Charitable contributions via payrollPost-taxConvenience; deductible if you itemize

Roth 401k vs Traditional 401k: The Tax Tradeoff

Traditional 401k (pre-tax): Reduces taxes now. You pay taxes when you withdraw in retirement. Better if you expect to be in a lower tax bracket in retirement.

Roth 401k (post-tax): No tax savings now. Withdrawals in retirement are completely tax-free. Better if you expect to be in the same or higher bracket in retirement, or if you're early in your career and in a low bracket now.

In 2026, the contribution limit for Roth 401(k) is the same as traditional: $23,500 ($31,000 if age 50+), or split between the two — but the combined total cannot exceed the limit.

Wage Garnishments: CCPA Limits

Federal law (Consumer Credit Protection Act) limits how much can be garnished from disposable earnings per pay period:

  • Ordinary debts (credit cards, medical bills): the lesser of 25% of disposable earnings or the amount by which disposable earnings exceed 30× the federal minimum wage ($7.25 × 30 = $217.50/week)
  • Child support/alimony: up to 50%–65% depending on whether the employee supports another family and how far behind they are
  • Federal student loans: up to 15%
  • Federal tax levies: the IRS table determines the exempt amount

How Post-Tax Deductions Show on Your Pay Stub

On a properly formatted pay stub, post-tax deductions appear after the tax section — below federal income tax, state income tax, and FICA. The order is typically: Gross Pay → Pre-tax deductions → Taxable wages → Taxes withheld → Post-tax deductions → Net Pay.
