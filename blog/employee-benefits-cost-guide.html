Employee Benefits Cost in 2026: What Small Businesses Actually Pay

For most small businesses, employee benefits add 20–40% to direct wage costs. Understanding the real cost helps you budget accurately, price your services correctly, and design a competitive benefits package without breaking the bank.

By FreePayrollCalc Editorial • Updated September 2026 • 8 min read

Health insurance: the biggest benefit cost

Average employer-sponsored health insurance costs in 2025–2026 (per BLS and KFF data):

  • Single coverage: ~$8,435/year total premium; employers pay ~$6,600 (78%), employees pay ~$1,835 (22%)
  • Family coverage: ~$23,968/year total premium; employers pay ~$16,357 (68%), employees pay ~$7,611 (32%)

Small employers (under 50 employees) not subject to the ACA employer mandate but often must offer insurance to attract talent. SHOP marketplace plans and Professional Employer Organizations (PEOs) can help small businesses access group rates.

401(k) and retirement matching

  • Most common match: 100% match up to 3% of salary = $1,800/year for a $60,000 employee
  • Generous match: 50% on up to 6% = $1,800/year for same employee
  • 2026 401(k) employee limit: $23,500 ($31,000 with 50+ catch-up)
  • 2026 combined limit (employee + employer): $70,000

Paid time off (PTO)

Average PTO for private industry in the US (BLS):

  • Less than 1 year service: 11 days (vacation) + 8 sick days = 19 total days
  • 5 years service: 15 days vacation + 8 sick = 23 days
  • 10+ years: 19 days vacation + 8 sick = 27 days

Cost of PTO = (annual days off ÷ 260 working days) × annual salary. 15 days PTO on a $60,000 salary = $3,462 in PTO cost.

Total employer cost per employee: the real number

$60,000 salary employee, full benefits package:
Gross wages: $60,000
Payroll taxes (FICA+FUTA+SUTA): $5,100 (~8.5%)
Health insurance (single): $6,600
401(k) match (3%): $1,800
PTO (15 days): $3,462
Dental + vision: $1,200
Life + disability: $600
Workers' comp (office): $210
Total employer cost: $79,972 — 33% above base salary
