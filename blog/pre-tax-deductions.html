Pre-Tax Deductions 2026: What They Are and How They Reduce Your Taxes

Updated October 2026 · FreePayrollCalc Research

Pre-tax deductions are amounts taken from your paycheck before income taxes are calculated. They reduce your taxable wages, which lowers both your federal and state income tax — sometimes by hundreds of dollars per month.

Pre-Tax vs Post-Tax: Key Difference

Pre-tax deductions (traditional 401k, HSA, FSA, health insurance): reduce current taxable income, but you pay taxes when you withdraw (for 401k) or the money is earmarked (FSA/HSA).

Post-tax deductions (Roth 401k, Roth IRA contributions, union dues, garnishments): taken after taxes — no immediate tax savings, but Roth withdrawals in retirement are tax-free.

Does 401k Reduce FICA?

No. Traditional 401(k) contributions reduce your federal and state taxable income but do not reduce FICA (Social Security or Medicare). HSA and FSA contributions do reduce FICA when made through payroll (employer plan) — that's a meaningful additional savings often overlooked.
