Why 401k Loans Cost More Than They Appear

The interest rate on a 401k loan is misleadingly appealing — "you're paying yourself." But the real costs are invisible on the statement: (1) Lost compounding on the borrowed amount during repayment. (2) Double taxation — you repay with after-tax dollars, then pay taxes again on withdrawals in retirement. (3) Job change risk — lose your job and the full balance may be due by your next tax filing date.
