South Dakota Paycheck Taxes in 2026

South Dakota has no state income tax, so your paycheck deductions are limited to federal income tax and FICA taxes (Social Security 6.2% + Medicare 1.45%). Every paycheck has two components: FICA taxes (fixed rates set by the IRS) and income tax withholding (based on your W-4 elections and filing status).

What's Deducted from a South Dakota Paycheck?

  • Federal income tax — ranges from 10% to 37% based on your annual income and W-4 form
  • Social Security — 6.2% on wages up to $176,100 (2026 wage base)
  • Medicare — 1.45% on all wages (plus 0.9% if wages exceed $200,000)
  • Pre-tax deductions — 401(k), HSA, health insurance premiums (reduce taxable income)
