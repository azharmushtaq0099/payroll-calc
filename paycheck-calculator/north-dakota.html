North Dakota Paycheck Tax Breakdown 2026

North Dakota uses a graduated (progressive) income tax with rates from 1.1%–2.27%. Only income above each bracket threshold is taxed at that bracket's rate — so your effective rate will be lower than 2.27%.

All North Dakota Paycheck Deductions Explained

  • Federal income tax — 10%–37% based on 2026 IRS tax brackets. Calculated after the $15,000 standard deduction (single filers). Adjust your W-4 to change withholding amount.
  • North Dakota state income tax — 1.1%–2.27%. Progressive brackets from 1.1% to 2.27%.
  • Social Security (FICA) — 6.2% on wages up to the $176,100 wage base. Your employer matches this amount. Once you reach the wage base, no further SS is withheld for the rest of the year.
  • Medicare (FICA) — 1.45% on all wages, no cap. An additional 0.9% applies to wages over $200,000. Your employer matches the base 1.45%.

How to Increase Your North Dakota Take-Home Pay

The most effective way to increase take-home pay in North Dakota is through pre-tax deductions: 401(k) contributions (up to $23,500 in 2026), HSA contributions ($4,300 single / $8,550 family), and pre-tax health insurance premiums. Each pre-tax dollar reduces both federal and state taxable income.

Example: contributing $5,000/year to a 401(k) in North Dakota reduces federal tax by ~$1,100 (22% bracket), saves $400+ in FICA, and reduces state tax by $0 — a total annual savings of $NaN just from the 401(k) contribution.
