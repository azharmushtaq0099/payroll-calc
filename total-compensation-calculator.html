What Benefits Are Worth the Most?

401k match: 100% instant return on contributed dollars — always maximize this first. A 4% match on $95K is $3,800/year in free money.

Health insurance: Often the largest hidden benefit. Employer-sponsored family coverage can be worth $20,000+/year. Comparing offers? Calculate the employer contribution difference, not just premiums you pay.

Equity (RSUs/options): Can dwarf salary at growth companies. At tech firms, $50K salary + $200K in RSUs vesting over 4 years = $100K total comp annually.
