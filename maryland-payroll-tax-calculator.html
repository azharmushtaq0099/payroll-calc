Maryland Payroll Tax Calculator 2026

Maryland has eight state income tax brackets from 2% to 5.75%, plus a mandatory county/city income tax ranging from 2.25% to 3.2% depending on where you live. The combined top rate for most Maryland residents is approximately 8.95%–8.99%. The standard deduction is $2,400 for single filers.

All Payroll Deductions in Maryland (2026)

  • Federal income tax: 10%–37% (2026 IRS brackets; single filer pays 22% bracket above ~$50,000 taxable income)
  • Maryland state income tax: 2%–5.75% + county 2.25%–3.2%
  • Social Security: 6.2% on wages up to $176,100 (2026 wage base)
  • Medicare: 1.45% on all wages (no cap); additional 0.9% on wages over $200,000

Notable Maryland Tax Features

Maryland's county income tax is unique — it is withheld by the employer along with state tax. Baltimore City imposes 3.2%, making it the highest local rate. Montgomery County imposes 3.2%; Prince George's County 3.2%. Most other counties are 2.25%–3.0%.

Maryland vs Neighboring States

For a $75,000 earner in Montgomery County, Maryland: state tax approximately $3,800 + county tax approximately $2,400 = total state/local income tax approximately $6,200/year (8.3% combined effective rate).

About This Calculator

This Maryland payroll tax calculator uses 2026 federal and state rates from IRS Publication 15-T and the Maryland Department of Revenue. Results are estimates based on the annualized withholding method. Actual withholding may differ based on W-4 elections, local income taxes, itemized deductions, tax credits, and pre-tax benefits. Always verify with your payroll provider or a tax professional for official payroll purposes.
