Parent PLUS Loan Repayment Strategy

At 9.08%, Parent PLUS loans are expensive. Key strategies to reduce total cost:

  • Pay during school: Interest accrues immediately, even while the student is enrolled. Making interest-only payments during the 4 years of college can save thousands.
  • Refinance privately: Parents with excellent credit can refinance to 5-7%, cutting total interest by 30-40%. Trade-off: lose federal protections.
  • PSLF after consolidation: If you work in public service, consolidate to a Direct Loan and enroll in ICR. 10 years of payments = full forgiveness, tax-free.
