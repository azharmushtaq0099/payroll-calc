What Makes a Good Invoice?

A professional invoice communicates clearly, looks credible, and reduces payment friction. These are the seven components every invoice should include:

  • Invoice number — sequential numbering (INV-001, INV-002…) makes it easy to track payments and reference specific invoices in correspondence.
  • Invoice date and due date — sets clear payment expectations. Net 30 (due 30 days after invoice date) is standard. Net 15 speeds up cash flow for service businesses.
  • Itemized line items — list each deliverable, product, or service separately with quantity, unit price, and line total. Clients are more likely to pay quickly when they can see exactly what they're paying for.
  • Tax (if applicable) — depending on your jurisdiction and industry, you may need to charge sales tax, GST, or VAT. Include the rate and calculated amount as a separate line.
  • Clear payment instructions — bank transfer details, PayPal address, or a payment link. Remove all friction between the client and the "pay" button.
  • Your logo and branding — even a simple wordmark adds professionalism and reinforces that this is a legitimate business document.
  • Late payment policy — optionally add your late fee policy in Notes (e.g., "1.5% per month after 30 days") to encourage on-time payment.

FAQ

Invoice generator questions

How do I create a free invoice online?
Fill in your business name, client details, and line items on this page. Set your tax rate and any discount. Then click "Print / Save PDF" and choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog. No account or email required.
Can I add a tax rate to my invoice?
Yes — enter your tax rate percentage in the Tax Rate field. The calculator automatically applies it after any discount. Works for US sales tax, Canadian GST, UK VAT, UAE VAT (5%), and any other percentage-based tax.
How do I download my invoice as a PDF?
Click "Print / Save PDF," change the print Destination to "Save as PDF," and click Save. The invoice is print-optimized — clean layout, no browser UI elements, correct margins.
Is there a limit to how many invoices I can create?
No limit. This is completely free and browser-based. Your data never leaves your device.
What should a professional invoice include?
Invoice number, invoice date, due date, your business info, client info, itemized line items with quantities and rates, subtotal, tax, total due, and payment instructions. This generator includes all of them.
