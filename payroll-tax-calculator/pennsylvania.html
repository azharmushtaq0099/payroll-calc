Pennsylvania Tax Guide

Pennsylvania Payroll Tax Overview 2026

Pennsylvania uses a flat 3.07% income tax rate. All taxable income is taxed at the same percentage, regardless of how much you earn. This makes payroll calculations straightforward.

Pennsylvania has a 3.07% flat state income tax, but local earned income taxes add 1%–4% depending on municipality.

Philadelphia has the highest local wage tax at 3.75% for residents — employers must register and withhold both state and city taxes.

FAQ

Pennsylvania payroll tax questions

What is the Pennsylvania income tax rate for 2026?
Pennsylvania uses a flat 3.07% income tax rate on all taxable income. Unlike progressive-tax states, the same rate applies whether you earn $30,000 or $300,000. This makes Pennsylvania payroll tax calculations simple and predictable.
What taxes do Pennsylvania employees pay on their paycheck?
Pennsylvania employees pay: (1) Federal income tax (based on W-4 withholding); (2) Social Security tax (6.2% on wages up to $176,100); (3) Medicare tax (1.45%, plus 0.9% on income over $200,000); (4) Pennsylvania state income tax (3.07% flat rate); (5) Local Earned Income Tax (1%–3.93% depending on municipality). The employer matches the Social Security and Medicare portions.
What payroll withholding form do Pennsylvania employees use?
Pennsylvania employees complete Pennsylvania REV-419 (if claiming exemption) for state withholding in addition to the federal W-4. Employers use both forms to calculate the correct combined withholding amount.
