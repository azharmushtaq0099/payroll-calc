The True Cost of Minimum Payments

Credit card companies set minimum payments low on purpose — it maximizes the interest you pay. On a $5,000 balance at 23% APR, making a $100 minimum payment takes 8+ years and costs over $8,000 total. Doubling your payment cuts both the time and interest by more than half.

Best Ways to Pay Off Credit Card Debt Faster

  • Balance transfer: Move to a 0% intro APR card. You have 12-21 months of zero interest to pay down principal.
  • Debt avalanche: Put every extra dollar toward the highest-rate card first. Mathematically optimal.
  • Debt snowball: Pay smallest balance first for motivational wins. Costs slightly more interest but higher completion rates.
  • Negotiate a lower rate: Call your issuer and ask. Customers with good payment history can often get 3-5% reductions.
