New Jersey Income Tax Rates 2026

New Jersey income tax: 1.4%–8.97%. Progressive brackets — only income above each threshold is taxed at that bracket's rate. Your effective rate will be lower than the top rate of 8.97%.

Effective Tax Rate vs Marginal Tax Rate

Your marginal rate is the bracket that applies to your last dollar of income. Your effective rate is the average across all brackets — always lower than the marginal rate. At a $65,000 salary in New Jersey, your marginal federal rate is 22%, but your effective total rate (federal + New Jersey state + FICA) is 19.7%.
