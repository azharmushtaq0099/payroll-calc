How to Calculate Burn Rate

Gross burn rate is total monthly cash outflow. Net burn rate subtracts monthly revenue. Runway = cash on hand ÷ net burn rate.

Example: $500,000 cash ÷ $50,000 net burn = 10 months runway. With $10,000/month revenue, net burn drops to $40,000 and runway extends to 12.5 months.

Runway Benchmarks

  • 24+ months: Strong — fundraise from a position of strength
  • 18–24 months: Standard — start fundraising if you need a Series A
  • 12–18 months: Watch closely — cut non-essentials if growth isn't accelerating
  • Under 12 months: Danger zone — begin cost cutting or fundraising immediately
  • Under 6 months: Crisis — cut aggressively and explore bridge financing now
