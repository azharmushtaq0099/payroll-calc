Florida Income Tax Rates 2026

Florida state income tax: No state income tax. Standard deduction: N/A.

Florida is one of nine states with no state individual income tax on wages or salary. Florida workers only pay federal income tax, FICA (Social Security and Medicare), and any local taxes applicable to their area.

All Payroll Deductions in Florida

  • Federal income tax: 10%–37% (2026 brackets, based on taxable income after $15,000 standard deduction for single filers)
  • Florida state income tax: No state income tax
  • Social Security: 6.2% on wages up to $176,100
  • Medicare: 1.45% on all wages (+ 0.9% on wages over $200,000)

While Florida has no income tax, employers in Florida pay a Florida Reemployment Tax (FUTA equivalent) with a standard rate of 2.7% for new employers on wages up to $7,000 per employee. This is an employer-only cost and does not affect employee take-home pay.

Florida vs Other States

Compared to a neighboring state like Georgia (5.49% flat rate), a Florida worker earning $65,000 keeps approximately $2,700–$3,200 more per year in take-home pay due to no state income tax. This advantage is often cited as a factor in Florida population growth.

About This Calculator
