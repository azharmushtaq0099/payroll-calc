How to Calculate Payroll from Hours Worked (2026)

Converting hours to a paycheck takes three steps: calculate gross wages, subtract FICA, subtract income tax withholding.

Step 1 — Gross Wages

Regular pay: hours up to 40/week × hourly rate. Overtime pay: hours over 40 × rate × 1.5. Add both for gross.

Step 2 — FICA (2026)

  • Social Security: 6.2% on wages up to $176,100/year
  • Medicare: 1.45% on all wages (plus 0.9% additional over $200K)

Step 3 — Federal Withholding

Uses the 2026 IRS Publication 15-T percentage method. Your gross is annualized, then the bracket rates (10% / 12% / 22% / 24%...) are applied, then divided back down to the pay period amount.

FAQ

Payroll hours questions answered

How do I calculate gross pay from hours?
Regular hours × hourly rate = regular pay. Overtime hours (over 40/week) × (hourly rate × 1.5) = overtime pay. Add both for total gross pay this period.
When does overtime start?
Under federal FLSA, overtime (1.5×) starts at 41+ hours in a workweek. California and Colorado also require overtime for 9+ hours in a single day. Some states require double-time (2×) for very long shifts.
What is FICA tax in 2026?
Employees pay 6.2% Social Security on wages up to $176,100, plus 1.45% Medicare on all wages. Total employee FICA is 7.65%. Employers match this amount separately.
How many hours per year is full-time?
40 hours/week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours/year. The ACA defines full-time as 30+ hours/week. After paid holidays and PTO, productive hours are typically 1,800–1,950 per year.
