Quick Answer

For a $75,000 salary in 2026, take-home pay ranges from $61,147 in no-tax states (TX, FL, NV, etc.) to as low as $52,550 in high-tax states (OR, MD). Federal income tax ($8,115) + FICA ($5,738) = $13,853 in federal deductions on every $75k salary regardless of state.

Methodology & Data Sources

Federal income tax: Calculated using 2026 IRS Publication 15-T Percentage Method Tables. Assumes single filing status, $15,000 standard deduction (2026 IRS adjustment), no dependents, no additional withholding.

Social Security: 6.2% on wages up to $176,100 (2026 wage base, per Social Security Administration). Wages above this threshold are not subject to Social Security tax.

Medicare: 1.45% on all wages. Additional 0.9% Medicare surcharge applies to wages over $200,000 (not applicable to salaries shown here).

State income tax: Based on each state's 2026 official withholding tables and rate schedules as published by each state's department of revenue or taxation. Standard deduction/personal exemption applied per state rules where applicable.

Assumptions: Single filer · Federal $15,000 standard deduction · No pre-tax deductions (401k, HSA, health insurance) · No adjustments for local taxes except MD county average and WA LTC · Figures are estimates for planning purposes. Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Oregon's take-home pay so low?

Oregon has one of the nation's highest state income tax rates — 4.75% to 9.9%, with the top bracket kicking in at relatively low income levels (9.9% applies above $125,000 for single filers). Combined with high federal taxes, Oregon workers on a $75,000 salary pay roughly $22,600 in total taxes, leaving 74% take-home.

Does moving to a no-income-tax state actually save you money?

On a $75,000 salary, a move from California to Texas saves approximately $3,575/year in state income taxes. However, cost of living differences — especially housing — often offset this. Texas property taxes (effective rate ~1.6%) are among the highest nationally, which offsets some income tax savings for homeowners.

How do pre-tax deductions affect take-home pay?

Pre-tax deductions (401k contributions, HSA, employer-sponsored health insurance premiums) reduce your taxable income, which lowers both federal income tax and state income tax. A $10,000 annual 401(k) contribution for someone in the 22% federal bracket saves approximately $2,200 in federal taxes alone. This dataset does not include pre-tax deductions — use our payroll tax calculator to model your exact situation.

Are these figures for bi-weekly or monthly pay?

The table shows annual and monthly (annual ÷ 12) take-home pay. Federal withholding can vary slightly by pay frequency due to how the IRS withholding tables work, but annual totals are consistent. For bi-weekly take-home, divide the annual figure by 26. For weekly, divide by 52.
