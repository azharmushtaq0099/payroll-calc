Alaska Salary Taxes Explained

  • Federal income tax: Graduated 10%–37% using 2026 IRS Publication 15-T brackets. $15,000 standard deduction for single filers reduces your taxable income first.
  • Alaska state income tax: $0 — Alaska collects no income tax on wages.
  • Social Security: 6.2% on wages up to $176,100 (2026 wage base). Employer matches.
  • Medicare: 1.45% on all wages. Additional 0.9% on wages over $200,000.
