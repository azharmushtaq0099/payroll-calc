The Most Efficient Way to Make Extra Payments

Extra payments are most powerful early in your loan when your balance is highest. A $200 extra payment in year 1 of a 30-year mortgage saves nearly twice as much as the same payment in year 15, because it prevents compounding on a larger balance for a longer period.

Key rule: Always specify "apply to principal" on extra payments. Some servicers apply extra funds to future scheduled payments instead, which doesn't reduce principal efficiently.
